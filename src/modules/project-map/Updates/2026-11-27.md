# Project Map Updates — November 27, 2026

## New: Analytics page

A new **Analytics** page shows reports on your projects and runs as charts.

### How to open it
- On the **Project Map** page, click **📊 Analytics** in the row of tabs.
- On the **Run Calendar** page, click **📊 Analytics** at the top.

You only see the button if your role has been given the Analytics page.

### What it shows
- **Key numbers** at the top: open projects, their total value, new orders, projects installed, runs, and open repairs. New orders, installed and runs also say how they compare with the period before.
- **Projects by status**: how many projects are in each status right now, in each status's own colour.
- **Orders received** and **Order value**: per week or per month.
- **Top dealers**: the dealers with the highest order value.

### How to use it
1. Pick a **Period** (for example "Last 3 months"), a **State** or a **Building category** at the top. Every chart changes straight away.
2. Point at a bar to see its exact number.
3. Click **Table** on any chart to see the same numbers as a list. Click **Chart** to go back.
4. Click **Reset** to go back to the last 12 months for everything.

Good to know:
- Orders are counted by the **Order Received** date, or by the day the project was entered when that date is empty.
- **Installed** counts projects that reached a finished status. It only goes back to the day status history started (shown on the page), so it grows over time.
- The State and Building category filters apply to projects; the **Runs** number always counts all runs.
